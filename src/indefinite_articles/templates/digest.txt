Plain-text part of the daily digest email.

Each "@@ name" line starts a section. $name placeholders are filled in by
mailer.build_digest. A line whose placeholders are all empty is left out, and
runs of blank lines are collapsed into one. Everything above the first section
(this text) is ignored.

Keep the "--" line before the instructions and the "digest-id:" line: replies
are cut off at either, so quoted text below them is never read as feedback.

@@ page
Today's $count recommendations

$note

$items

--
$instructions

digest-id: $digest_id

$debug

@@ item
$position. $title
   $source$exploratory
   $url
   Why: $reason
   $blurb

@@ exploratory
  · something different

@@ debug
-- debug --
$lines
